WORLD MODELS
World models are models that infer world state from observations and predict how this state evolves given actions. This allows to train policy in virtual episodes.
Here we describe two prominent models, DreamerV3 and TWISTER, and their components.

https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.04104
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.04416

Key components of DREAMER/TWISTER

ht = Fϕ(ht-1, zt-1, at−1) - context encoder, e.g. RNN or transformer.
zt ∼ qϕ(zt | ot) - TWISTER observation encoder, models the posterior distribution of zt from the current observation. In DreamerV3 it is zt ∼ qϕ(zt | ot, ht).
For AC-CPC, TWISTER additionally computes z't ∼ qϕ(zt | augment(ot)) from an augmented view of the observation.
hat zt ∼ dϕ(zt | ht) - dynamics prior: predicts the distribution of the stochastic world state from the context, which already encodes previous states and actions.
st = {ht, zt} - posterior model state used by the policy, reward and continuation heads. The TWISTER CPC implementation instead uses the prior feature {ht, hat zt} as its predictor input.
ot ∼ pϕ(ot | zt) - TWISTER observation decoder. In DreamerV3 the decoder is ot ∼ pϕ(ot | st).
         ekt = uk(z't) - projection of the augmented posterior state into the embedding space by the CPC head for horizon k.

hat ekt = wk({ht, hat zt}, at:t+k-1) - predicted future embedding from the prior feature and the action sequence; for k=0 there are no explicitly concatenated future actions.
         rt ∼ rϕ(rt | st) - predicted reward for the current transition
ct ∼ cϕ(ct | st) - predicted episode continuation/termination

losses:
The prior hat zt ∼ dϕ and posterior zt ∼ qϕ are aligned with KL divergence. DreamerV3 and TWISTER use balanced prior/posterior KL terms with stop-gradient on the opposite branch.
qϕ and pϕ are trained with observation reconstruction negative log-likelihood together with the KL terms.
wk and uk are trained with contrastive InfoNCE loss between predicted embeddings hat ekt and projected augmented targets ek(t+k) = uk(z't+k).

It is also possible to learn other useful signals such as rewards and episode termination.

Action Conditioned (AC-CPC)
This is a TWISTER component that jointly trains the predictor wk, target projection uk, augmented observation encoder and TSSM/prior representation.
This is an architecture that allows compressing high-dimensional observations into useful representations.


RSSM stands for Recurrent State-Space Model. This is a part of dreamer that constitutes the world model. In TWISTER it is TSSM - transformer instead of recurrent model.
There are two key differences:
DreamerV3 aligns the prior hat zt ∼ dϕ(zt | ht) with the posterior zt ∼ qϕ(zt | ot, ht), while TWISTER uses an observation-only posterior zt ∼ qϕ(zt | ot).
This is similar to recursive Bayesian estimation or Bayesian filtering and to inference in a hidden Markov model.
TWISTER adds the AC-CPC contrastive objective to its reconstruction, KL, reward and continuation losses.

Another minor difference is that the decoder in DreamerV3 is ot ∼ pϕ(ot | st).

As an alternative design, one can concatenate multiple observations or use a Dreamer-style posterior qϕ(zt | ot, ht) for AC-CPC, but this is not how the released TWISTER implementation works.
Both implementations use discrete categorical stochastic states zt, although related world models can use other latent distributions.

##
## Dreamer-style world model state update
##
##############################


h0=0, posterior z0 = qϕ(h0, o0)
s0 = [ h0, z0] -> a0
h1=Fϕ(z0, h0, a0), prior hat z1 = dϕ(h1)
posterior z1 = qϕ(h1, O1)
s1 = [h1, z1] -> a1
h2=Fϕ(z1, h1, a1)

conceptually information flow:
z0 = q(h0, o0) -> h1 = F(h0, z0, a0) -> z1 = q(h1, o1) -> predict a1 from s1
s0 -> a0,
s1 -> a1
and for the released TWISTER AC-CPC implementation starting from the prior state at t=1:
hat e10 = w0(h1, hat z1) - conditioned by a0 through h1 and hat z1
hat e12 = w1(h1, hat z1, a1)
hat e13 = w2(h1, hat z1, a1, a2)
with positives hat e10 <-> u0(z'1), hat e12 <-> u1(z'2), hat e13 <-> u2(z'3), where z'k is encoded from an augmented observation o'k
in TWISTER the CPC target sequence z't:t+K is computed from augmented observations o't:t+K; each z'k = qϕ(z | augment(ok)) is encoded independently, not from the whole observation window

##############################
##
## TWISTER losses:
##
##############################

observation reconstruction:
hat ot = decoder(zt)
observation_loss = -log pϕ(ot | zt)
# MSEDist: equivalent to pixel reconstruction MSE with fixed variance
# gradients: decoder -> posterior zt -> observation encoder

prior/posterior alignment:
prior = dϕ(hat zt | ht)
posterior = qϕ(zt | ot)

kl_prior = max(
    free_nats,
    KL(detach(posterior) || prior)) # trains dynamics prior/TSSM to predict the posterior

kl_post = max(
    free_nats,
    KL(posterior || detach(prior))) # trains observation encoder toward the dynamics prior

free_nats = 1.0
AC-CPC:
for horizon k = 0..K-1:
    x_tk = wk([ht, hat zt, at:t+k-1])
    y_tk = uk(z't+k)
    z't+k = qϕ(z | augment(ot+k))

    cpc_k = InfoNCE(x_tk, y_tk)
    
    
hat olat_k = sensor_latent_head(e_k0) #  sensor embedding reconstruction, deterministic, computed from anchor cpc only
MSE(G(detach(anchor_CPC_t)), detach(enc(o_t)))


TWISTER world-model loss =
    1.0 * observation_loss
    + 1.0 * reward_loss
    + 1.0 * continue_loss
    + 0.5 * kl_prior
    + 0.1 * kl_post
    + 1.0 * sum_k(cpc_weight_k * cpc_k)

    + KL(detach(q_t) || p_t) # with free nats
